^YSyntax: ^Ngender <male/female/none>
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Sets the pronouns used for you in object scripting (%psp/%pop/%pap/%ppn - see help object) and a handful of other messages (eg "away"). Male uses he/him/his, female uses she/her/hers, and "none" uses singular they/them/their/theirs - not "it", which is for objects, not people.

^RRestrictions:^N

None

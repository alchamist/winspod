^YSyntax: ^Nconverse
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Toggles converse mode. While on, anything you type that isn't a recognised command, social, alias, or punctuation-prefixed chat (' or ;) is said aloud to the room instead of getting a "Huh?" - useful if you keep forgetting to prefix your speech. Type converse again to leave it.

^RRestrictions:^N

None

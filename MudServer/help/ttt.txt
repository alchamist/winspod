^YSyntax: ^Nttt <player> or ttt <cell 1-9>
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Challenges another online player to a game of tic-tac-toe, or - once a game is under way - plays a move.

"^Gttt <player>^N" challenges them; if they've already challenged you, the same command accepts instead. The challenger plays X and goes first. Cells are numbered like a phone keypad:

 1 | 2 | 3
---+---+---
 4 | 5 | 6
---+---+---
 7 | 8 | 9

"^Gttt <number>^N" plays that cell on your turn. "^Gttt^N" on its own reshows the board. Your record shows up on "examine" once you've played a game.


^RRestrictions:^N

None

^YSyntax: ^Nblackjack <hit/stand>
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Play a hand of blackjack against the dealer. "^Gblackjack^N" on its own deals a new hand (two cards for you, one shown for the dealer) if you don't already have one in progress. "^Gblackjack hit^N" draws another card; "^Gblackjack stand^N" stops there and lets the dealer play out their hand (drawing to 17 or more).

Closest to 21 without going over wins. No credits are staked - your record shows up on "examine" once you've played a hand.


^RRestrictions:^N

None

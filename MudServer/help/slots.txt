^YSyntax: ^Nslots
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Pulls the lever on the slot machine. Costs 5 credits to play, into a shared pot that grows every time someone doesn't win:

Two matching symbols     - your 5 credits back
Three 7s                 - the whole pot
Three cherries            - half the pot
Three of anything else   - a quarter of the pot
No match                 - your 5 credits join the pot

The pot is shared across everyone playing - see how much is in it (and your own balance) after every pull. Wins/losses don't show up on "examine" - slots is pure chance, not a record worth keeping.

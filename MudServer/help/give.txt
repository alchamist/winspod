^YSyntax: ^Ngive <object name> <player>
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Gives an object from your inventory to another player, wherever they are on the system, as long as they are online. Objects that are unique to a player can only be held one at a time, and the recipient cannot exceed their maximum carry weight.

^RRestrictions:^N

None

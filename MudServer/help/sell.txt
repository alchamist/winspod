^YSyntax: ^Nsell <object name> <price>
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Lists an object from your inventory for sale in the current room at the given price. Unlike drop, you keep ownership until someone actually buys it - use unsell to take it off the market again. See help shop to see what's for sale in a room, and help buy to purchase something.

^RRestrictions:^N

None

^YSyntax: ^Ncreatesoc <name> <type>
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Creates a new social with the given name. <type> is one of:

simple - never takes a target
complex - target is optional
private - target is required

The name cannot match an existing command or another social, must start with a letter, be at least 3 characters long, and may otherwise only contain letters, numbers, and _ - ~. Use setsoc to fill in its messages. See help socials for the full reference.

^RRestrictions:^N

Player must be a resident and have builder privs

^YSyntax: ^Niacga
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Toggles whether the server sends a Telnet Go-Ahead signal (IAC GA) after your prompts. Only useful for old half-duplex terminals that wait for it before sending anything back - modern clients, and the browser client, both ignore it. Off by default.

^RRestrictions:^N

None

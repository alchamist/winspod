^YSyntax: ^Nnopager
^YAliases: ^NNone

^PEffect:^N

Toggles paging of long output. When on (the default), anything longer than one screen pauses with a "-- More --" prompt - press enter to see the next page, or type q to stop and discard the rest. Uses your terminal's reported height (NAWS), same as line wrapping uses its reported width.

^RRestrictions:^N

None
